EMBODIED INTELLIGENCE

Siyuan Liu

Fourth-year Ph.D. student

Institute of Automation,
Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA)

I study how robots learn to move and manipulate through vision-language-action (VLA) models, reinforcement learning, and musculoskeletal systems.

My previous research focused on manipulation in musculoskeletal systems. I am now exploring how large-scale models can enable manipulation and coordinated loco-manipulation.
